Stable states

Relaxes from structured seeds and many random starts, keeps every stationary point and merges states related by a lattice rotation/reflection or a global flip. Click an entry to load it; sweeping again extends the catalog.

Transitions

For every stable state, bisects the smallest perturbation — per core (±), per normal mode (±) and along random directions — that escapes its basin, and where it lands. Escapes into uncatalogued minima seed a follow-up sweep. Extra probes spot-check that each ray is monotone (⚠ otherwise). Nodes sit by energy; edge width ∝ count, label = energy climbed along the ray.
